Getting there

Island Packers, 1691 Spinnaker Dr, Ventura. Be there by 7:00 Friday. Campers check in an hour early, and anyone not ready 30 minutes before the boat doesn't get on.

Boutcher and I are driving the kayaks down.

Parking

Pay at the Port District kiosk on the walkway next to the Island Packers building, grab a parking pass at check-in, and park in the Whale Lot. It's about $10 a day, so carpool.

Food and drinks

What the boat will take

60 lbper person, not counting water
45 lbmax for any one bag
5 galmax per water jug
  • Soft coolers only. No YETI-style coolers, and nothing over 45 lbs.
  • No carts or wagons.
  • Fuel canisters get handed over separately at check-in. Don't pack them.
  • Anything with a lithium battery rides in your day pack. That includes dive lights, headlamps, and vapes.
  • Knock the dirt and seeds off your boots and gear before you come.
